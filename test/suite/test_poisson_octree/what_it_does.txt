each deck runs createpts / poisson_disk / 3d_box with h_lookup octree (an h-adapted octree for the h field and the neighbour search, with a maximal fill) on a box with a known h field: a point source, constant h, other seeds and k factors, the h field in small batches, the octree file written and read back; po06 and po07 run the same fields with the old hex_grid lookup for comparison. The checker measures the spacing against the exact h, maximality, time and memory, and the tets' angles; the pictures show a slice of the octree and the vertices near it. po10 checks the edge buffer's lattice on the six box faces on a large box (made without the hex brick of the whole box that needed about 1.5 GB). po11 to po13 check face_lattice adaptive, the face vertices following h: their spacing, the gap to the sampled vertices, the Voronoi points of the tets on the faces inside the box and positive coupling coefficients, against the uniform lattice (po12).
